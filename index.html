Table Tennis Strategy

How it works:
  1. Click Edit to set player names for the selected team.
  2. Reorder the 'Our Team' and 'Opposition Team' lists to match your lineup.
  3. Drag items in 'Player Rankings' to combine both teams and rank by skill.
  4. 'Match Predictions' shows outcomes based on the current team orders.
  5. 'Optimal Team Order' suggests the best order for your team.
